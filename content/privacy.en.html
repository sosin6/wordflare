The short version

Wordflare processes your videos on your own device. Your video, its audio and its captions are never uploaded to our servers. We don't sell personal data and we don't use advertising cookies.

What we process

  • Your videos: handled entirely inside your browser. The AI model is downloaded to your browser (from Hugging Face's public servers) and cached there.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: we use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not identify you. It records things like pages visited, country, device type, which buttons are used, and technical error messages, so we can fix bugs and improve the product.
  • Visits: Vercel Web Analytics counts page views, referrers, country and device type without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Product events and feedback: a few anonymous events (for example: captions generated, video exported, technical errors, which Pro features were clicked) and the text you type in the feedback form are stored in a private spreadsheet (Google Sheets) so we can fix bugs and decide what to build. Please don't include personal information in your feedback.
  • Waitlist email (optional): if you ask to be notified about Wordflare Pro and tick the consent box, we store your email, the feature you were interested in, your language and the date in a private spreadsheet (Google Sheets). We use it only to tell you when Wordflare Pro launches and to send your discount.

Legal basis and your rights

We process waitlist emails based on your consent, and anonymous statistics based on our legitimate interest in running and improving the service. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your email at any time, or withdraw your consent, by writing to {{cfg.CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe.

Third parties

  • Vercel (website hosting and visit statistics)
  • Umami Cloud (anonymous analytics)
  • Google (private spreadsheet for waitlist emails and product events; Google Fonts)
  • Hugging Face and jsDelivr (download of the AI model and its code to your browser)

Retention

Waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, or until we stop offering the waitlist. Anonymous statistics are kept in aggregate.

Children

Wordflare is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their email addresses.
